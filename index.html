the problem

This is your brain every time you try to cook.

Then it clicked.

Let Nufii do that for you.

We turn an AI-built, personalized nutrition plan into a real grocery kit, real daily meals, and a low-effort habit — not another plan you have to figure out how to actually follow.

Most diet apps stop at the plan. You tell them your goal, they hand you a PDF of macros and meal ideas, and then it's entirely on you to turn that into a shopping list, buy the right quantities, figure out substitutions, and decide — every single night — what you're actually cooking. That gap is where almost everyone falls off.

Nufii closes it. Tell it the diet you want to follow and how many people you're cooking for, and it builds a plan within careful, non-clinical bounds, converts that plan into a kit sized to your table, and gives you recipes made only from what's in that kit — no improvising, no extra grocery run. Want something beyond the kit? You can shop for more food or gym gear. Nothing else, for now — we're keeping the first version tight on purpose.

And to be upfront: this is not clinical or medical nutrition advice, and it's not built yet for complex allergy or medical-condition cases — that's deliberate, until trust and safety are proven out.
